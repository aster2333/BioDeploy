Assay and delivery questions

How long does a run take?

Planning target: Standard 21 days / Rush 14 days after digital design, synthesis feasibility, reagent availability and lab schedule are confirmed. Synthesis delays and reruns may extend delivery.

Can I order at these prices?

These are demo prices. The calculator downloads an estimate, but does not take payment or submit an experiment.

What kinetic parameters do I receive?

Compatible assays provide continuous traces, initial rates, nonlinear KM / Vmax fits and QC. kcat and kcat/KM require an established active enzyme concentration. Poor fits and below-detection results retain null parameters and reasons.

What if expression or activity is not detected?

No expression may mean no catalytic measurement. Rates below the assay detection limit are retained as negative results, not zero activity. QC failures keep raw signals without trusted fitted constants. Real rerun scope and fees require agreement.

Will I receive physical protein?

These packages deliver data. Physical protein delivery, scale-up, synthesis, cloning, purification, specialty reagents, taxes and shipping are outside the base estimate.

Where will experiments run?

The planned federated wet-lab mesh spans APAC and global bio-hubs. GenScript and Twist Bioscience remain listed; facilities in Switzerland and China are to be confirmed. Project-specific availability, data access and regional restrictions are confirmed before work begins.

What can I try in this demo?

Compare synthetic enzyme variants, inspect progress curves and fitted kinetics, export JSON or CSV with controls, and estimate assay costs. Real uploads, ordering and API access are not enabled.
