07Questions

A few things worth knowing.

Are these prices available to order?

No. All prices, quantities, and timelines are illustrative. Saving an estimate only downloads a local JSON; it creates no order or payment.

Screening or full characterization?

The demo screening scope ranks binding from two concentrations. Affinity characterization uses four and reports kinetics only when the data and QC support them. A precise Kd is not guaranteed for every sequence.

What if a protein does not express or bind?

These are distinct outcomes. No expression means no binding measurement; no binding can be a valid negative result. QC failure needs review. Before a real order, rerun scope, fees, and responsibilities must be agreed separately.

Will I receive physical protein?

This demo is data-only. Physical protein, scale-up, shipping, target procurement and their costs are outside the estimate.

Does language decide where my experiment runs?

No. Language, delivery region and currency are separate. The network is illustrative; real routing, data access and regional constraints need confirmation.

Are the data and API real?

All shown measurements and provenance are synthetic. JSON and CSV downloads work locally. API snippets describe a proposed contract and make no network calls. No private research input is collected.
